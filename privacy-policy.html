Privacy Policy

1 Introduction

Welcome to Hexasolv ("we," "our," or "us"). Hexasolv is a full-stack software development and digital growth agency based in Sargodha, Punjab, Pakistan. We operate the website https://www.hexasolv.com (the "Site").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or contact us for services. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our Site.

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:

Personal Data You Provide:

  • Name and email address (when you fill out our contact form or newsletter subscription)
  • Phone number (when you contact us via WhatsApp or the contact form)
  • Project details and business information you share when requesting a quote or consultation
  • Messages and correspondence you send to us

Automatically Collected Data:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on our Site
  • Referring URLs and search terms used to find our Site
  • Device information (operating system, screen resolution)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide project consultations
  • To send you requested quotes, proposals, and service information
  • To send newsletters and marketing emails (only if you have opted in)
  • To improve our website content, user experience, and services
  • To analyze website traffic and usage patterns using analytics tools
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activities

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4 Third-Party Services & Disclosure

We may share your information with trusted third parties only as necessary to operate our Site and deliver our services:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Site. Google may collect your IP address and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense: We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Vercel (Hosting): Our website is hosted on Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel may process request logs including IP addresses. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp / Meta: When you contact us via WhatsApp, Meta's data practices apply. See WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Email Service Providers: If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email may be stored with our email service provider.

We may also disclose your information if required to do so by law or if we believe such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect our rights, or prevent harm.

5 Google AdSense & Advertising

We participate in the Google AdSense program. Google AdSense uses cookies and web beacons to serve ads on our Site based on your interests. This allows Google and its partners to show you ads that may be relevant to your interests on our Site and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out
  • Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

6 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your browser or device.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Site to function properly (e.g., theme preference: light/dark mode).
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics to collect anonymous data about Site traffic and usage.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect some functionality of our Site. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. See your browser's help documentation for instructions.

7 Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

Our website is served over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption). All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

8 Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at contact@hexasolv.com.

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete such information from our records.

9 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your personal data for direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time where we rely on consent to process your data.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Ads: Opt out of personalized advertising as described in Section 5.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@hexasolv.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

10 Links to External Sites

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., our social media profiles, LinkedIn, WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page when we make changes.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Your continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
